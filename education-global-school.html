Global School of Agrivoltaics

Through workshops, training programs, and online courses, we connect people across different countries to share knowledge and best practices - creating the Global School of Agrivoltaics. We have also participated in events like the AgriVoltaics World Conference to teach the fundamentals of agrivoltaic design, operation, and policy.[3] This knowledge exchange accelerates the adoption of best practices worldwide, creating a community of trained individuals who can adapt agrivoltaics to their local needs.
