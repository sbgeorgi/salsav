HOW A SOURCE IS SCORED
Fit is 100 points. You judge four parts and send them as `fit`; the database derives the rest. Never send fit_score.
- fit.mission (35): core, strong, adjacent, weak, none. fit.activity (15): research_demo, capacity, other, mismatch. fit.geography (15): home, region, global, outside, excluded. fit.precedent (5): direct, similar, none. Each is {level, why, quote, url}. Use the level definitions in the brief's `rubric`.
- Eligibility (20) from ua_eligibility: eligible 20, eligible_with_partner 13, unknown 7, invitation_only 3, unlikely 0. Award (10) from award_max_usd, the largest single award: unknown 4, under 200k 0, under 500k 6, under 1M 8, 1M or more 10.
- Coded gates, first match rejects: ua_eligibility unlikely; largest single award under USD 200,000; mission level none; total under 40. A vetted row needs fit.mission.quote and a one-sentence vet_note, else it is a hidden candidate.
- The radar lists FUNDERS; the crawler then watches each for its next call. Only these four gates reject on fit. Never reject because the funder does not name universities (UA counts in any role; send unlikely only when the funder's rules exclude US universities in every role, silent or unclear = unknown), because the call is closed, a one-off or has no announced round (cycle closed_unknown, no next_deadline), or because it is invitation-only (cycle invite_only when fit is strong).
- Reject a non-funder (no money reaches applicants: technical assistance, research program, news, aggregator) with reject_kind not_a_funder, and a funder that stopped for good with reject_kind ended. Every other real funder goes in as vetted with honest levels; the gates decide.
- A dated call counts on the dashboard only when its deadline is 14+ days out. A sooner or past deadline is dropped from the row, never guessed forward; the funder stays listed. An expired call of an annual program: cycle annual_recurring, no next_deadline.
Read status, notes and reject_reason of each return. The database may store a row lower than you sent it; report what it stored.

EVIDENCE RULES
- A quote is text copied without edits from the official page, fetched this run: `python supabase/grants/tools/fetch_text.py URL --grep "regex"`. Never quote WebFetch output. One sentence, 20 to 240 characters, no ellipsis.
- Prefer quotes from the page you register as `url`: the database re-checks quotes against the crawler's copy and flags any it cannot find.
- No quote, no claim: without an eligibility quote send ua_eligibility unknown; without an award quote send amount_confidence estimated or unknown. Never guess a deadline.
- award_max_usd is USD for one award, not the program budget. Other currencies: leave the number out, put the amount in award_note.
- An aggregator snippet is not the official page. A blocked or thin page proves nothing: say so (fetchability bot_protected or js_required), lower confidence, fill in nothing.
- Themes come from the brief's theme_vocab only.

RUN TARGET: at least 5 NEW funders listed this run

- Looking at 5 leads is not the target. Listing 5 is. Keep discovering (step 7) until 5 are listed or you have examined 80 candidates this run.
- The target never loosens the evidence. Send honest levels and let the gates decide; a lead that fails is registered as rejected and you move to the next one.
- A run that ends under 5 says so in the summary: how many were listed, how many candidates were examined, and which buckets and search angles ran dry.
